Rules

This contains the rules of the Global Demonlist+. This explains how it accepts records and places levels.

WARNING: THESE RULES ARE SUBJECT TO CHANGE. IF YOU WANT SOMETHING TO CHANGE, YOU CAN CONTACT THE MODERATORS IN THE SERVER.

Acceptance of Records

Before you submit a record, you must of course know the rules.

  • Must be posted on YouTube or Medal.
  • Google Drive or private sharing can also be used, but only in extreme cases.
  • If the video is edited, raw footage is required.
  • The sound of clicks must be audible.
  • Clicks must be genuine and not fake sounds.
  • Clicks may be hidden, but raw footage is then required.
  • If using a version lower than 2.209, a cheat indicator must be shown.
  • Alternatively, the cheat indicator may be shown on the endscreen, but the endscreen must be included in the video.
WARNING FOR MODERATORS: ANY PRIVATE INFORMATION YOU SEE FROM RAW FOOTAGE IS STRICTLY PROHIBITED FROM BEING SHOWN. IF YOU ARE CAUGHT SAYING ANYTHING PEOPLE SHOULDN'T SEE YOU WILL BE KICKED FROM THE TEAM ON THE SPOT.

If the proof is insufficient, moderators may require:

  • CPS Counter
  • FPS Counter
  • Clock
  • Handcam
  • Input Overlay

Bans

If a player is suspected of cheating, the record will simply be rejected.

If multiple cheated records are submitted, the player will receive a temporary ban.

However, if a player is found to have any form of bigotry (such as racism, transphobia, or homophobia), the player will be banned from both the server and the leaderboard.

Staff Roles

List Leader / Owner
The owner of the list.

List Co-Leader / Co-Owner
Self-explanatory.

List Moderator
A moderator of the list. May have no affiliation with the server team and can correct mistakes made by List Helpers.

List Helper
Not a moderator, but can accept or reject records.

List Suggestor
A list admin who has nothing to do with moderation, but can suggest features and improvements.

Additional Notes

  • The SpaceUK Method is allowed on this list.
  • Do not report players solely for using the SpaceUK Method.
  • Staff may request additional proof at any time.
